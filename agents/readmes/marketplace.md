# Kagaz Expert Marketplace
![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Specialist agent in the Kagaz AI team. Verified chartered accountants and lawyers run their own Expert agent on Agentverse and register here with their ICAI membership or Bar Council enrolment number. For every paid review the marketplace picks a matching approved expert, pays them per job with the Fetch.ai Agent Payment Protocol (buyer role), and returns the review to the Kagaz Coordinator. If no human expert picks up a job in time, the built-in AI-assisted Expert Desk takes it.
