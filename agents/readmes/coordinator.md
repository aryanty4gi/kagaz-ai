# Kagaz AI - Any official notice, turned into action
![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Got a traffic challan, income tax notice, electricity disconnection notice, bank letter or a suspicious SMS? Paste it here.

Kagaz coordinates a team of five specialist agents to:
1. **Check if it is real**: flags fake e-challans, fake tax refunds and KYC scams
2. **Explain it** in plain English or Hindi, grounded in the actual rules
3. **Draft your reply** and give the exact official steps
4. **Remind you** before the deadline
5. **Get an expert review**, paid agent-to-agent with the Payment Protocol

**Try:** paste "VAHAN ALERT: Your vehicle has a pending e-challan of Rs.500. Pay now: https://echallan-parivahan.top/pay"

Supported: traffic e-challans, Income Tax 143(1), 139(9), 148, electricity disconnection (Electricity Act s.56), SARFAESI 13(2) bank notices.

*General information, not legal advice.*
