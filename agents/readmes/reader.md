# Kagaz Reader
![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Specialist agent in the Kagaz AI team. Extracts notice type, issuer, amount, deadline, reference numbers, links and phone numbers from Indian official notices and SMS. Deterministic extraction for hard facts, enriched by the ASI:One LLM.

## Use me on my own

This agent works on its own on ASI:One: mention it and send your message, no other agent needed. Paste any notice or SMS, or upload a photo or screenshot. I reply with the notice type, sender, amount, deadline (with days left), reference numbers, links and phone numbers, plus a one-line summary, and warn you if it looks like a scam.

**Try:** `Electricity disconnection notice from BSES: amount Rs 6,240 due, consumer no. 4123987650, pay within 15 days or supply will be disconnected.`

Part of the Kagaz AI team: for the full guided flow (scam check, explanation, reply draft, reminders, paid expert review) chat with **@kagaz**.
