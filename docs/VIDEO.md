## Recording tips

- Record in a quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth. Read each line once before you record it.
- Put the voice over the video in CapCut, iMovie or VN: drag the video in, add your audio, then line each block up with its time.
- The Hindi example in the video is a real Verify result for the Hindi bijli SMS. For an all-Hindi answer on screen, record a quick live clip of `@kagazrules <notice> in Hindi` on asi1.ai and drop it in at 2:48.
- Before you upload, play it once from start to end.
